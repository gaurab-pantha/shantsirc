Our story

Shanti Science, ICT & Robotics Club (SSIRC) is a student community at Shree Shanti Namuna Secondary School in Manigram, Rupandehi. Members learn programming, electronics and robotics by building real things, join workshops and events, and lead projects together.

A Board of Directors of ten students leads the club.
