Why partner with us?

Our members are students who want to learn robotics, coding and technology by doing. Working with SSIRC is a chance to support them and meet the next generation of makers in Rupandehi.

Ways to work together

Workshops and training

Run a session for our members, like our Aviation & Drone and 3D-printing workshops.

Events and competitions

Support or co-host an event, competition or exhibition.

Mentorship

Share your experience with students learning robotics, coding and electronics.

Tools and spaces

Help with equipment, lab time or venues for our activities.
